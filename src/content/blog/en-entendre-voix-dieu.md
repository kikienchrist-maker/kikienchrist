# How to Hear God's Voice in the World's Noise

> **"My sheep hear my voice; I know them, and they follow me."** — John 10:27

Many Christians ask: _"Is it God speaking to me, or my own thoughts?"_ Here are 5 biblical criteria to discern God's voice.

## 1. God's Voice **always aligns with His written Word**

> "Your word is a lamp to my feet, a light on my path." (Psalm 119:105)

**Test:** Does what you "hear" contradict a clear biblical principle? → **Not God.**

| Source        | Characteristic                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **God**       | Never contradicts the Bible     |
| **Our flesh** | Justifies our desires           |
| **The enemy** | Contradicts, accuses, confounds |

## 2. God's Voice **brings peace (even in the test)**

> "I leave you peace, I give you my peace." (John 14:27)

| Voice            | Interior Effect                               |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **God**          | Peace, assurance, clarity (even if difficult) |
| **Anxiety**      | Rush, fear, confusion                         |
| **Condemnation** | Shame, discouragement, "you're not enough"    |

> **Note:** Conviction of the Holy Spirit = "This hurts God, we repent." Hope. Condemnation = "You're worthless, abandon." Despair.

## 3. God's Voice **aligns with the fruit of the Spirit**

> "The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, mercy, fidelity, gentleness, self-control." (Galatians 5:22-23)

**Question:** Does this direction produce these fruits in me and around me?

## 4. God's Voice **is confirmed by the community**

> "In the multitude of advisors, there is success." (Proverbs 15:22)

**Practical:** Share what you believe you hear with a mature spiritual mentor, pastor, or mature friend. Isolation = danger.

## 5. God's Voice **leads to Christ and His glory**

> "It is I who am the way, the truth, and the life." (John 14:6)

**Ultimate Test:** Does this direction bring me closer to Jesus? Does it give glory to Him?

## 🔍 3 Common Pitfalls

| Trap                                            | Reality                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **"God told me to... "** to justify a desire    | God never contradicts His Word                              |
| **Seeking spectacular signs**                   | God often speaks through "a gentle whisper" (1 Kings 19:12) |
| **Waiting for a 100% certainty before obeying** | Faith = obey without seeing everything (Hebrews 11:1)       |

---

## 🛠 Practical Exercise: "The Test of the 3 Filters"

Before acting on an impression:

1. **Biblical Filter:** Is it in line with God's Word?
2. **Peace Filter:** Is there peace from the Holy Spirit (Colossians 3:15)?
3. **Council Filter:** Does a mature brother/sister confirm it?

> **If all 3 are ✅ → Proceed with faith.**  
> **If one is ❌ → Wait, pray, consult.**
