# Spiritual Warfare: Praying with Authority (Ephesians 6) — A Practical Guide

> **“Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand firm against the devil’s schemes.”** — Ephesians 6:11

Prayer isn’t just “talking to God”—it’s **fighting in the supernatural**. Paul gives us the complete equipment to **stand firm** and **move forward**.



## 1. The Reality of the Battle (Context)

| Biblical Truth | Reference |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Real enemy**: the devil, demons, principalities | Ephesians 6:12 |
| **Spiritual battle**: not against flesh and blood | 2 Corinthians 10:3-4 |
| **Jesus has already conquered**: Colossians 2:15 | John 16:33 |
| **We fight FROM victory, NOT FOR victory** | Romans 8:37 |

> **Key**: We do not pray _to obtain_ authority—we pray _from_ the authority already given in Christ.



## 2. The Armor of God — 6 Pieces + Prayer (Ephesians 6:14–18)

| Piece | Verse | Function | Prayer to Put On |
| ------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Belt of Truth** | v.14a  | Holds everything together; integrity, Word | “Lord, gird my loins with Your truth. May Your Word be my standard.” |
| **Belt of Truth** | v.14b  | Protects the heart; Christ’s righteousness (not our own) | “Clothe me in Your righteousness. Keep my heart pure before You.” |
| **Shoes of the Gospel of Peace**  | v.15   | Mobility; readiness to proclaim; stability | “Give me feet ready to carry Your peace wherever I go.” |
| **Shield of Faith** | v.16   | Extinguishes **all** flaming arrows | “My faith is in You. I raise the shield: every arrow is extinguished in the name of Jesus.” |
| **Helmet of Salvation** | v.17a  | Protects the mind; assurance, hope | “Crown me with my salvation. Protect my thoughts and my identity in Christ.” |
| **Sword of the Spirit = Word of God** | v.17b  | **Only offensive weapon**; Rhema (living word) | “Put Your Word in my mouth. May it be alive, sharp, and effective.” |
| **Prayer at all times** | v.18   | **Communicate** with the Commander; watch and persevere | “Teach me to pray in the Spirit, at all times, for all the saints.” |

## 3. Morning Routine: “Putting on the Armor” (3 min)

> **Standing, aloud**: (Faith comes from what we _hear_ — Romans 10:17)

```
“In the name of Jesus Christ:
I PUT ON the BELT OF TRUTH—Your Word is my truth.
I PUT ON the BREASTPLATE OF RIGHTEOUSNESS—Your righteousness covers me.
I SHEATHE my feet with THE GOSPEL OF PEACE — I carry Your peace.
I TAKE UP the SHIELD OF FAITH — Every arrow is extinguished.
I PUT ON the HELMET OF SALVATION — My thoughts are on You.
I GRASP the SWORD OF THE SPIRIT — Your Word in my mouth.
I PRAY in the SPIRIT, at all times, for all the saints.
I STAND FIRM. I OVERCOME. In the name of Jesus. Amen.”
```

## 4. 3 Biblical Prayers of Authority (Templates)

### A. Prayer for **Protection** (Psalm 91 + Ephesians 6)

> **For yourself / family / home / ministry**

> “Father, in Jesus’ name, I cover [name / home / ministry] with the blood of Jesus.
> I clothe each one in the full armor of God.
> I raise a shield of faith around [name]—no arrow can strike them.
> I bind every spirit of fear, confusion, sickness, division, and accident.
> I send Your angels to guard [name] in all his ways (Ps 91:11).
> May Your peace guard their hearts and minds in Christ Jesus (Phil 4:7).
> In the name of Jesus, the Victor. Amen.”

### B. Prayer of **Deliverance / Stronghold** (2 Corinthians 10:4–5)

> **To break a pattern of sin, addiction, oppression, or obsessive thoughts**

> “In the name of Jesus Christ, I stand in the authority He has given me (Luke 10:19).
> I identify the stronghold: [name it: e.g., “fear of the future,” “addiction to…,” ‘resentment toward…’].
> I DESTROY this stronghold by the power of the Holy Spirit.
> I OVERTHROW every argument and every lofty thing that exalts itself against the knowledge of God.
> I TAKE EVERY THOUGHT CAPTIVE TO THE OBEDIENCE OF CHRIST.
> I PROCLAME the truth: [contrary verse: e.g., “God has not given me a spirit of fear”—2 Tim. 1:7].
> I GIVE THANKS for the victory already won at the cross.
> In Jesus’ name. Amen.”

### C. Prayer for **Advancement / Conquest** (Joshua 1:3 + Matthew 16:18–19)

> **For ministry, evangelism, a new season, territory**

> “Lord, You said, ‘Every place where the sole of your foot treads, I have given it to you’ (Joshua 1:3).
> I advance in Your name into [territory: neighborhood, city, nation, professional field, family].
> I bind the spirit of [identified resistance] and I loose [blessing: peace, revival, open door, provision].
> I proclaim: “The gates of Hades will not prevail against Your Church” (Matt. 16:18).
> I use the keys of the Kingdom: I bind on earth = bound in heaven; I loose = loosed (Matt. 16:19).
> May Your Kingdom come; may Your will be done on [territory] as it is in heaven.
> In the name of Jesus, the Victor. Amen.”

## 5. 7 Common Strongholds + Verses of Overcoming

| Stronghold | The Enemy's Lie | Biblical Truth (Sword) |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fear / Anxiety** | “You’re going to fail / Something bad will happen to you” | “God has not given me a spirit of fear” (2 Tim 1:7) |
| **Rejection / Worthlessness**     | “You’re not good enough / Nobody loves you” | “I am a son/daughter of God, an heir ” (Rom 8:16–17) |
| **Anger / Bitterness**     | “You’re right to seek revenge” | “Forgive one another, just as God has forgiven you” (Eph 4:32)     |
| **Addiction / Bondage** | “You’ll never be able to break free” | “Whoever the Son sets free is truly free” (John 8:36) |
| **Illness / Pain**     | “God doesn’t heal anymore / This is your cross”   | “By His wounds we are healed” (Isa 53:5) |
| **Deprivation / Poverty**     | “You’ll never have enough” | “My God will meet all your needs” (Phil 4:19) |
| **Isolation / Loneliness**  | “You are alone, abandoned” | “I am with you always” (Matt. 28:20) |

## ⚠️ 5 Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Correction |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Praying _against_ demons without being _clothed_** | First: full armor (Eph 6:11) |
| **Mentioning the devil more than Jesus** | 10:1 ratio—10x “Jesus” for every 1x “enemy” |
| **Believing that authority = our words** | Authority = **position in Christ** (Eph 2:6) |
| **Neglecting personal repentance** | “If we confess our sins... ” (1 John 1:9) |
| **Acting alone** | “Where two or three are gathered...” (Matthew 18:20) — Team up! |
