# Daily Prayer: 7 Keys to a Fruitful Spiritual Life

> **“Pray without ceasing.”** — 1 Thessalonians 5:17

Prayer is not an option for Christians; it is our **spiritual breath**. Yet many of us struggle to maintain a consistent daily prayer life. Here are 7 biblical keys to establishing this vital habit.



## 1. Choose a Set Time and Place

Jesus used to go off **“very early in the morning to a deserted place”** (Mark 1:35). Consistency builds a habit.

> **Practical Tip**: Set aside 15 minutes every morning before doing anything else. Create a “prayer corner” (chair, Bible, notebook).



## 2. Use the A.C.T.S. Framework

A simple framework to help you avoid going in circles:

| Letter | Meaning    | Example |
| ------ | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **A**  | **Adoration**    | “Lord, You are holy, almighty, faithful...” |
| **C**  | **Confession**   | “Forgive me for [specific sin]...” |
| **T**  | **Thanksgiving** | “Thank You for [yesterday’s blessing]...” |
| **S**  | **Supplication** | “I pray to You for [specific need]...” |



## 3. Praying the Word (Lectio Divina)

Turn a verse into a personal prayer:

> **Psalm 23:1** → “Lord, You are my shepherd. I shall not want. Teach me to trust You in times of need.”



## 4. Keep a prayer journal

Write down: the date, a Bible verse, your requests, and the answers. **Seeing your prayers answered strengthens your faith** (Psalm 66:16–20).



## 5. Praying for Others (Intercession)

“Pray for all people” (1 Timothy 2:1). List: family, church, government leaders, the lost, the sick.



## 6. Accepting Times of Drought

David cries out, “My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?” (Psalm 22:1). **A dry season does not mean God is absent**—it is often during these times that He deepens our roots.



## 7. End by Listening

Prayer is a **dialogue**. After you’ve spoken, **be silent for 2–3 minutes**. Write down whatever comes to mind (a thought, a verse, a feeling).
