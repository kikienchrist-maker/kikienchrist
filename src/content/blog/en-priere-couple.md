# Praying as a Couple: 7 Keys to Shared Spiritual Intimacy

> **“For where two or three are gathered in my name, there am I among them.”** — Matthew 18:20

Praying **together** isn’t just “doubling” your prayer—it’s **inviting Jesus into the space between you**. Couples who pray together have **94% fewer divorces** (Barna study). Here’s how to get started.



## 1. 🕐 Set aside sacred time (Non-negotiable)

| Obstacle | Solution |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| “No time”    | **5 minutes** upon waking OR at bedtime = 35 min/week    |
| “Kids / noise” | Wake up 15 min before them / After they go to bed / In the car |
| “Not in the habit”  | **Anchor**: after coffee, before dinner, sitting on the edge of the bed |

**Rule**: This time is **sacred**—no phones, no “to-do” discussions, no arguments.



## 2. 🤝 Hand in Hand (Physical Contact)

> “A threefold cord is not quickly broken.” (Ecclesiastes 4:12)

**Why**: Touch releases oxytocin (the “bonding hormone”) and symbolizes unity before God.

**Tried-and-true positions**:

- Hand in hand, eyes closed
- Forehead to forehead (maximum intimacy)
- Husband wrapping his arms around his wife (protection)
- Standing, embracing (strength)



## 3. 📋 Shared Prayer List (Living Board)

**Formats**: “Our Prayer” notebook / Magnetic fridge board / App (Notion, Trello).

| Column | Examples |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gratitude** (Yesterday) | “Thank you for reconciliation,” “Thank you for the promotion” |
| **Personal Need** (Me) | “Patience with the kids,” “Wisdom for a work decision”  |
| **Spouse’s Need** (You)  | “Back healing,” “Job interview” |
| **Family / Home** | “Children: obedience,” “House sale,” “Neighbors” |
| **Answers to Prayers** (Archive)  | ✅ “Pregnancy,” ✅ “Mom’s healing,” ✅ “New job”   |

**Weekly ritual**: Sunday evening—review, check off, give thanks, add.



## 4. 🙏 Pray **for** one another (Not just _with_ one another)

> “Pray for one another, so that you may be healed.” (James 5:16)

**Take turns**: One person prays **aloud for the other** (5 min), then switch.

**Example**:

> “Lord, I entrust [first name] to You. You know his/her heart, his/her weariness, and his/her fear regarding [topic]. I ask You for [specific request]. May he/she feel Your love today. In Jesus’ name.”

**Effect**: Being _heard_ praying for someone = a powerful factor in building intimacy.



## 5. 🙌 Gratitude First (An Antidote to Criticism)

> “Enter His gates with thanksgiving.” (Psalm 100:4)

**Rule 3:1**: **3 expressions of gratitude** for **1 request**.

| Gratitude (examples) | Request |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1. Your patience last night     | Help me listen without interrupting |
| 2. Your support for [project] | Heal my back |
| 3. The coffee this morning | Wisdom for budgeting |

**Why**: Gratitude **changes the atmosphere** of the heart before bringing up needs.



## 6. 🤫 Shared Silence (Listening as a Pair)

> “Know that I am God.” (Psalm 46:11)

**Practice**: 2–3 minutes with **hands clasped, in total silence**. No words. Just **presence**.

**What happens**: Often, the same impression, verse, or image comes to both → **confirmation**.



## 7. 🏃 Persevere When Things Get Tough

| Phase | Realities | Key |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Beginning (0–3 months)**    | Awkward, forced, forgotten | **Schedule**: Set aside 5 min/day |
| **Routine (3–12 months)** | Mechanical, weariness | **Vary**: methods (see below)     |
| **Crisis** | Anger, silence, “it’s not working” | **Don’t stop**—pray _even_ when angry |
| **Depth (1+ year)**  | Natural, intimate, visible fruit | **Pass it on**: pray with children |

> **Testimony**: _“For 6 months, we prayed ‘mechanically.’ Then one evening, my husband cried in my arms. That’s when everything changed.”_ — Claire, married for 15 years

## 🛠 5 Ways to Mix Things Up (Break the Routine)

| Method | Description |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Psalm of the Day** | Take turns reading 1 Psalm → pray based on what it inspires |
| **2. Attributes of God** | Every day: “Lord, You are [Faithful / Holy / Good / Just...]” |
| **3. Names of Jesus** | “Good Shepherd / Bread of Life / Light / Prince of Peace...” |
| **4. Promises for the Couple** | Ephesians 5:25 / 1 Corinthians 13 / Colossians 3:14 |
| **5. “ACTS” Prayer as a Couple**   | A (Adoration) together → C (Confession) individually → T (Thanks) together → S (Supplication) individually |

## 📅 30-Day “Praying Couple” Challenge

| Week | Focus | Duration |
| ------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1 | Gratitude Only | 5 min |
| 2 | Personal Needs (take turns) | 7 min |
| 3 | Family / Children / Home | 10 min |
| 4 | Silence + Listening | 5 min silence + 5 min sharing |

**Rule**: **No “zero day.”** If you miss a day → start over the next day (no guilt).
