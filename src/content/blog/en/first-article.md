# 7 Powerful Prayers to Start Your Day

Starting your day in God's presence changes everything. Here are 7 Scripture-based prayers to help you begin each morning with intention and faith.

## 1. The Prayer of Thanksgiving (Psalm 118:24)

> « This is the day that the Lord has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it. »

**Prayer**: *Heavenly Father, I thank You for this new day. It is a gift from You. Help me see Your hand in every detail today. May my heart overflow with gratitude. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: Psalm 118:24, 1 Thessalonians 5:18

---

## 2. The Prayer for Wisdom (James 1:5)

> « If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives generously to all without reproach, and it will be given him. »

**Prayer**: *Lord, I come before You acknowledging my need for wisdom. Give me discernment for today's decisions. May Your thoughts become my thoughts. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: James 1:5, Proverbs 3:5-6

---

## 3. The Prayer of Protection (Psalm 91:1-2)

> « He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High will abide in the shadow of the Almighty. I will say to the Lord, 'My refuge and my fortress, my God, in whom I trust.' »

**Prayer**: *Father, I place myself under Your protection today. Cover me with Your wings. Let nothing touch me without Your permission. You are my rock and my fortress. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: Psalm 91, Ephesians 6:10-18

---

## 4. The Prayer for the Holy Spirit (Luke 11:13)

> « If you then, who are evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will the heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask him! »

**Prayer**: *Holy Spirit, fill me anew this morning. Guide my steps, illuminate my words, direct my thoughts. May I bear fruit today. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: Luke 11:13, Galatians 5:22-23

---

## 5. The Prayer of Intercession (1 Timothy 2:1)

> « First of all, then, I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all people. »

**Prayer**: *Lord, I bring before You my family, friends, colleagues, my country. May Your will be done in their lives. Give me an intercessor's heart. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: 1 Timothy 2:1, Colossians 4:2-4

---

## 6. The Prayer of Mission (Matthew 28:19-20)

> « Go therefore and make disciples of all nations... »

**Prayer**: *Father, use me today to witness Your love. Give me opportunities to speak of You. May my life reflect Your light. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: Matthew 28:19-20, Acts 1:8

---

## 7. The Prayer of Surrender (Proverbs 3:5-6)

> « Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and do not lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths. »

**Prayer**: *Lord, I surrender this day to You. My plans, my worries, my joys — all are Yours. Lead me on Your paths. Your will be done. In Jesus' name, Amen.*

**Key Verses**: Proverbs 3:5-6, Romans 12:1-2
