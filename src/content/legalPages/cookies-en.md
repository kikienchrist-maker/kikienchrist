  # Cookies Policy

  This policy explains what cookies are, how **Kiki en Christ** uses them, the types of cookies that are stored, and how you can manage your preferences.

  ## 1. What is a cookie?

  A **cookie** (or tracker) is a small text file stored on your terminal (computer, smartphone, tablet) during a visit to a website. It allows the site to recognize your terminal during subsequent visits.

  Cookies can be:
  - **Session cookies** : deleted when the browser is closed
  - **Persistent cookies** : remain until expiration or manual deletion (max 13 months according to ePrivacy)

  ## 2. Responsible for the processing

  **Kiki en Christ** — contact@kikienchrist.com

  ## 3. Cookies used

  ### a) Necessary cookies (always active)
  Essential to the functioning of the site. **Cannot be deactivated.**

  | Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
  |-----|----------|-------|------|
  | `preferred-lang` | Store the language chosen | 1 year | Persistent |
  | `kikienchrist-consent` | Store your cookie preferences | 1 year | Persistent |
  | Vercel/Netlify Session | Security, CSRF, load balancing | Session | Session |

  ### b) Audience measurement cookies (optional — consent required)
  **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** — Anonymized data, IP masked.

  | Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
  |-----|----------|-------|------|
  | `_ga` | Identify unique users | 14 months | Persistent |
  | `_ga_<ID>` | Persist the session state | 14 months | Persistent |
  | `_gid` | Distinguish users (24 hours) | 24 hours | Persistent |
  | `_gat` | Limit request rate | 1 minute | Persistent |

  **Legal basis** : Consent (RGPD Art. 6.1.a + ePrivacy Art. 5.3)  
  **Subcontractor** : Google LLC (USA) — Data Processing Amendment signed, contract terms.

  ### c) Marketing cookies (reserved — currently unused)
  No marketing cookies are deposited today. This category is reserved for future campaigns (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Ads). If activated: consent required.

  ### d) Functional cookies (reserved — currently unused)
  No third-party functional cookies are deposited. Reserved for future features (chat, video player, advanced forms). If activated: consent required.

  ## 4. Managing your consent

  ### During your first visit
  A **cookie banner** appears (bottom of the page) with 3 choices:
  - **Accept all** → All cookies (analytics + future marketing/functional)
  - **Reject all** → Only necessary cookies
  - **Personalize** → (If reactivated) Granular choice by category

  ### Modify your preferences later
  - Click on the **⚙️** icon (desktop) or **Preferences** (mobile) at the bottom right
  - Or delete the cookie `kikienchrist-consent` via your browser

  ### Withdraw consent
  At any time. The withdrawal does not affect the legality of the previous processing.

  ## 5. Automatic deletion of third-party cookies (if refusing analytics)

  If you refuse audience measurement, our script automatically deletes the existing GA4 cookies:
  - `_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gid`, `_gat` → deleted via `document.cookie` with expiration passed

  ## 6. Consent Mode v2 (Google)

  We use **Google Consent Mode v2**:
  - By default: `analytics_storage: denied`, `ad_storage: denied`
  - If consent to analytics: `analytics_storage: granted`
  - This allows GA4 to model conversions without cookies if refused

  ## 7. Your rights

  See [Privacy Policy](/fr/confidentiality/) — Section "Your rights".
