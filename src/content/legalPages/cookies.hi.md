# Cookies Policy

## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your terminal (computer, mobile) during a visit to a site. It allows your browser to recognize your device during subsequent visits.

## Types of cookies used

### 1. Strictly necessary cookies (always active)

| Name             | Purpose                               | Duration |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------- |
| `preferred-lang` | To remember your preferred language   | 1 year   |
| `cookie-consent` | To remember your consent choices      | 1 year   |
| `fp_ref`         | Affiliate attribution (FirstPromoter) | 30 days  |

These cookies are essential for the functioning of the site. You cannot disable them.

### 2. Analytical cookies (require your consent)

| Name           | Provider               | Purpose                            | Duration  |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------- |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics (GA4) | Audience measurement, user journey | 14 months |
| `_gid`         | Google Analytics       | User distinction                   | 24 hours  |

With **Consent Mode v2**: if you refuse, no personal data is sent to Google (cookieless mode, modeling).

### 3. Marketing cookies (require your consent)

| Name     | Provider      | Purpose                                    | Duration |
| -------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------- |
| `_fbp`   | Meta Pixel    | Facebook/Instagram ad conversions tracking | 90 days  |
| `fp_ref` | FirstPromoter | Affiliate sales attribution                | 30 days  |

## Managing your consent

- **GDPR banner** : displayed on first visit (or if consent expired)
- **"Personalize" button** : choose by category
- **Withdrawal** : at any time via the "Manage cookies" link at the bottom of the page
- **Duration** : consent is remembered for 1 year, then re-requested

## How to disable cookies via your browser

- **Chrome** : Settings > Privacy > Cookies
- **Firefox** : Options > Privacy > Cookies
- **Safari** : Preferences > Privacy
- **Edge** : Settings > Cookies

_Note: Disabling cookies may break certain features (language, shopping cart, login)._
