  # Privacy Policy

  **Kiki en Christ** places a primary importance on the protection of your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how we use it, and your rights in accordance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU 2016/679)** and the **Law on Information Technology and Liberty**.

  ## 1. Data Controller

  **Kiki en Christ**  
  [Complete address]  
  Email: contact@kikienchrist.com  
  Represented by: [Name of the publication director]

  ## 2. Data Collected

  ### a) Navigation Data (via cookies — see [Cookie Policy](/fr/cookies/))
  - IP Address (anonymized via GA4)
  - Visited Pages, Session Duration, Referrer
  - Device Type, Browser, Language, Screen Resolution
  - Approximate Country/Region (IP Geolocation)

  ### b) Newsletter Subscription Data
  - Email (mandatory)
  - First Name (optional)
  - Preferred Language (deduced or chosen)
  - Explicit Consent (double opt-in via Netlify Forms / MailerLite)

  ### c) Contact / Form Data
  - Name, Email, Message
  - Metadata: Date, Time, IP (for anti-spam)

  ### d) Purchase Data (via third-party platforms)
  **We do not store any financial data.** Transactions are processed by:
  - **Lulu** (paper books) — their privacy policy applies
  - **Payhip** (ebooks) — their privacy policy applies
  - **Google Play** (audiobooks) — their privacy policy applies

  ## 3. Purposes & Legal Basis

  | Purpose | Data | Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6) | Duration of Retention |
  |----------|---------|---------------------------|----------------------|
  | Audience Measurement (GA4) | Data Navigation | Consent (Art. 6.1.a) | 14 months (GA4) / 1 year (cookie consent) |
  | Newsletter Subscription | Email, First Name, Language | Consent (Art. 6.1.a) | Until unsubscribed |
  | Contact Response | Name, Email, Message | Legitimate Interest (Art. 6.1.f) | 1 year after last exchange |
  | Cookie Preferences | Preferences | Legal Obligation (Art. 6.1.c) | 1 year (cookie `kikienchrist-consent`) |
  | Security / Anti-spam | IP, Metadata | Legitimate Interest (Art. 6.1.f) | 30 days |

  ## 4. Recipients / Subcontractors

  Your data may be transferred to:
  - **Vercel** (hosting, access logs, Edge Functions) — EU/USA, contract terms
  - **Google Analytics (GA4)** (audience measurement) — USA, Data Processing Amendment signed
  - **Netlify** (forms, serverless functions) — EU/USA, DPA signed
  - **MailerLite** (emailing newsletter) — EU, DPA signed
  - **Lulu / Payhip / Google Play** (book sales) — their respective policies

  No transfer outside the EU without appropriate guarantees (contractual terms, adequacy decision).

  ## 5. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 15-22)

  You have the following rights:
  - **Right to Access** — Obtain a copy of your data
  - **Right to Rectify** — Correct inaccurate data
  - **Right to Erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — Delete your data
  - **Right to Limit** — Temporarily halt processing
  - **Right to Portability** — Receive your data in a structured format
  - **Right to Object** — You can object to processing (e.g., marketing)
  - **Right to Withdraw Consent** — For newsletter / cookies

  **Exercise of Rights** : contact@kikienchrist.com (response within 30 days max)

  ## 6. Security

  - HTTPS/TLS 1.3 across the entire site
  - Strict CSP (Content Security Policy)
  - No storage of passwords (no user accounts)
  - Limited access to data to authorized individuals

  ## 7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

  See our [Detailed Cookie Policy](/fr/cookies/).
